Use case: game mask texture. Asset type: 1024x1024 single-channel style pattern mask for one floor tile; the game multiplies it with a team colour (vermilion, indigo, yellow or green) when the tile is painted. Image 1 shows the intended look of painted tiles (the red/blue/yellow/green tiles with pale cloud and deer motifs); use it only as motif reference.
Pure black background with the motif drawn in flat white/light grey only, no other colours. Motif: a Han-dynasty cloud-and-animal brocade (云气动物纹锦) — flowing scrolling cloud bands (云气纹) with one small leaping deer, in a refined woven-brocade line style, clean bold shapes readable when the tile is small on a phone screen. Motif covers about 35–45% of the area, evenly distributed; keep a calm border region. The pattern must tile seamlessly left/right and top/bottom so neighbouring painted tiles join into one continuous brocade.
Flat, no shading, no texture noise, no gradients, no text, no frame, no perspective.
